## <span style="color:#B88945">CURRENT FOCUS — LEDGERTRACE</span>

**LedgerTrace** is my current focus: a reconciliation workbench for comparing business records and finding discrepancies that deserve attention.

It is designed around a practical question: **do the invoice, purchase order, and receipt agree?**

- **Compare records:** inspect related financial and operational data side by side.
- **Surface discrepancies:** identify mismatched prices or quantities, missing records, and potential duplicates.
- **Keep findings traceable:** connect a flagged difference to the records that support it.

[**Open LedgerTrace**](https://github.com/DT-09/LedgerTrace) · [Source code](https://github.com/DT-09/LedgerTrace)
