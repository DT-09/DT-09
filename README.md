## / 00 — Operating thesis

I build and investigate systems at the intersection of **AI, software infrastructure, and assurance**.

My focus is the gap between what an intelligent system *can* do and what it *should be permitted to do* — and how to make those boundaries testable, observable, and explainable.

> **Build with intent. Verify the boundaries.**
